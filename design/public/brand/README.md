# NearZoop brand

A loud, street-level mark for offers that are happening *right now*.

## The mark

A hand-drawn black tile, printed slightly off-register with a golden sheet behind it.
The italic **Z** is cut straight out of the tile. The imperfection is the point:
it feels pasted up on a shop window, not generated.

- Tile: `#1C1B19`
- Offset print / Z: `#FFC93B`
- Signal orange: `#FB4B32`
- Paper: `#F4ECDF`
- Ink: `#1C1B19`

## Rules

- Give the mark clear space equal to the **Z** on every side.
- Minimum size: 24 px wide for the tile, 120 px for the horizontal lockup.
- Do not recolour the tile or the Z, add outlines, glows, or gradients.
- Do not rotate, stretch, or re-space the wordmark. Tight tracking is part of it.
- On orange, prefer the reversed lockup. On cream/white, use the ink lockup.

## Type

- Display: **Inter Black (900)**, tracking `-0.048em`, line height `0.90`
- Body: **Inter SemiBold (600)**
- Utility / status: **IBM Plex Mono Medium (500)**, uppercase, tracking `0.16em`
