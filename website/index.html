HOW IT WORKS

Three taps to the good stuff.

No feeds, no algorithms, no doom-flicking. Just your patch of the city, and what's on offer in it right now.

01

Point at your patch

Let the app see your location only while it's open — or search any locality by name or PIN. Gariahat, Bhowanipore, 700019… your lanes are the feed.

02

Dial your radius

Slide from 0.5 km up to 10 km. Offers only come from the streets inside the red dotted circle you confirm — near means near.

03

Bookmark the good ones

Read every offer like a sticker sheet, tap into its shop, and bookmark what's worth the walk. Your saved rail keeps score.

FOR THE SHOPS

Your counter, on every map nearby.

Post an offer from your shop and it lands on the phones of everyone poking around your lanes — no feed to fight, no ranking to buy.

Coupons are claimed in the app and verified at the counter — in person, quick look, done.

Post a live offer

One screen: what's on, how much, till when. It goes up as a sticker pin on the streets map.

Shoppers bookmark you

Anyone passing through can browse without an account — your sticker is seen, tapped, saved.

Verified at the counter

Customers claim in the app; you confirm it's them and honour the deal. At day's end, the tally is simple.
